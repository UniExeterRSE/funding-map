pathway (Unix, HPC fundamentals, C/C++, MPI/OpenMP) with mentoring and meet-ups, ending at the VI-HPS Tuning Workshop
in Durham, 17–20 May 2027, travel and accommodation paid. No expertise needed, but you must be able to attend.
