A 1–3 month placement in a dRTP team at Imperial, King's, UCL or Westminster, covering 80% of salary. Open to staff
of those four, to PhD students and postdocs not otherwise employed during it, and to part-time or unemployed dRTPs
anywhere in the UK. Reviewed monthly; placements must finish by October 2027.
